Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy applies to "rclone personal app", a private application used only by its owner to transfer files to the owner's own Google Drive.

Information we access

The app accesses the owner's Google Drive through the Google Drive API, only to upload and manage files that the owner chooses to transfer.

Information we collect and store

We do not collect, store, sell or share any user data. Access tokens are saved only in the rclone configuration file on the owner's own computer.

Third parties

No data is shared with any third party. Data goes only between the owner's computer and Google Drive.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
