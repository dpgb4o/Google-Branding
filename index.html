rclone personal app

This is a private application used by its owner to upload and back up personal photos and videos to the owner's own Google Drive with rclone.

What it does

The app connects to Google Drive through the Google Drive API, using the owner's own Google account, to copy files from the owner's computer to the owner's Drive.

Who can use it

The app is for personal use only. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Your data

The app does not collect, store or share data with anyone. Read the privacy policy.
